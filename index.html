Cade's 28th: The Chicken Chase

  • 🐔The birthday boy is the chicken. Cade puts on a chicken head, takes the pot, and gets 30 minutes to hide inside one bar from a list we pick ahead of time. Once he's in, he can't leave.
  • 💸Everyone puts $10 to $20 in the pot and splits into 4 teams. Nobody sees the bar list until the teams are released.
  • 🍺Every bar your team walks into that doesn't have the chicken, you order a drink, finish it, and take a team photo before moving on.
  • 🤫Each team keeps track of the bars it has checked in its own chat. Nobody tells the other teams which bars are empty, where they're headed, or that they found him.
  • 🍻When you find the chicken, you stay and drink from the pot with him. The last team to find him buys everyone a round.
